# Code of conduct

Treat contributors with respect. Discuss ideas and evidence; do not attack people. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing and publication of private information are not acceptable.

Give specific, constructive feedback. Respect different experience levels and credit other people's work. Keep discussions relevant and avoid unsolicited promotion.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation in proportion to the behavior. Use the repository's **Report abuse** feature for private reports to GitHub. Do not post personal information in a public issue. A maintainer involved in a dispute should not be its sole reviewer when another maintainer is available.
